Questions

Does it work on my university's lecture platform?+
It's tested on Panopto and Echo360, including lectures embedded in Canvas or Moodle course pages. It also works on YouTube and most other HTML5 video players. If it doesn't work on your platform, email us.
Why does the player still show 1x?+
That's normal: the extension controls the speed, not the player's own menu. On Panopto and Echo360 the player's menu may keep showing 1x while the lecture plays at your speed.
Is it allowed to speed up lectures?+
The extension only changes the playback speed in your own browser, like the speed setting on YouTube. It doesn't download, record or change anything on the platform. Any rules your course has about recordings still apply.
Can I understand anything at 3x?+
It depends on the lecture. In a UCLA study (Murphy et al., Applied Cognitive Psychology, 2022), comprehension dropped only slightly at 1.5x and 2x, and more at 2.5x. Go fast on the parts you already know and slow down for the hard ones: that's what checkpoints and the A-B loop are for.
Do you collect my data?+
No. The extension has no analytics and doesn't send what you watch anywhere. Its only network request is the check of a Pro license key. This website counts visits with Vercel Web Analytics, without cookies. See the Privacy Policy.
How do I activate Pro?+
Start the 7-day free trial. Your license key arrives by email: open the extension's popup, paste the key and click Activate.
Can I cancel?+
Yes, anytime, from the link in your Lemon Squeezy receipt email. Cancel during the 7-day trial and you pay nothing. After a payment you have 14 days to ask for a refund: just email us.
I bought the old lifetime license. Do I need to pay again?+
No. Lifetime licenses bought before the yearly plan stay Pro forever.
