FAQ

Does it work with my university's platform?⌄
SpeedUp is tested on Panopto and Echo360, including lectures embedded in Canvas or Moodle course pages. It also works on YouTube and most HTML5 video players. If your university uses another platform and it doesn't work, write to us.
The player still shows 1x?⌄
Normal: SpeedUp controls the speed, use D/S or the popup. On Panopto and Echo360 the player's own speed menu is ignored while SpeedUp is active, so it may keep showing 1x while the lecture plays at your speed.
How fast can I watch a lecture?⌄
There's no 2x cap: most students study at 1.5x–3x. SpeedUp goes up to 16x, but at very high speeds some lecture players can't load the video fast enough and fall back to 1x.
Do you collect my data?⌄
No. The extension has no analytics and doesn't track what you watch: settings, stats and checkpoints stay in your browser. The only network request is the check of a Pro license key. This website counts visits with Vercel Web Analytics, without cookies — see the Privacy Policy.

Can I cancel?⌄
Yes, anytime, from the link in your Lemon Squeezy receipt email. Cancel before the end of the 7-day trial and you pay nothing. After that, Pro is $4.99/year and stays active until the end of the paid year.
I bought the old lifetime license. Do I need to pay again?⌄
No. Lifetime licenses bought before the yearly plan stay Pro forever.
