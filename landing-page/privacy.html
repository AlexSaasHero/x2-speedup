Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Complete Privacy & Zero Data Collection

At X2 SpeedUp, we believe your browsing habits and viewing data belong solely to you. The X2 Chrome Extension does not collect, record, track, transmit, or sell any personal data, browsing history, video URLs, or usage telemetry to external servers.

2. 100% Local Execution

All video playback speed adjustments, HTML5 media manipulations, and custom hotkey listeners operate entirely locally within your web browser. No external scripts or remote analytics services are loaded during video playback.

3. Use of Local Storage

The extension uses Chrome’s built-in local storage (chrome.storage.local) strictly to store:

  • Your preferred default playback speed setting.
  • Your validated License Key to enable premium extension features.

This data remains stored locally on your device and is never shared with third parties.

4. Third-Party Payment Processing

Payments and license generation are handled securely by our merchant of record, Lemon Squeezy. When you purchase a license key, your billing information is processed directly by Lemon Squeezy under their privacy policy. We do not store or process your financial data or credit card numbers.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, feel free to reach out to our team at support@x2speedup.com.
