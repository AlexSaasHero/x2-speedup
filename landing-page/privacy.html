Privacy Policy

Privacy Statement for the SpeedUp Video Pro browser extension and the website x2-speedup.vercel.app

Effective Date: October 2, 2026 | Last Updated: November 15, 2026 (Extension version 1.9.0)

🔒 Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy covers two different things, which handle data differently:

  • The SpeedUp Video Pro browser extension ("the Extension") — works 100% locally in your browser and contains no analytics or tracking of any kind (Section 1).
  • This website, x2-speedup.vercel.app ("the Website") — uses cookie-free, aggregated visitor statistics (Section 3).

🚫 1. The Extension: Zero Tracking

The Extension does NOT collect, record, monitor, transmit, or sell personal data or browsing activity.

  • It does NOT include any analytics, telemetry, web beacons, or tracking scripts.
  • It does NOT send the websites you visit, video titles, or video URLs anywhere.
  • It does NOT sell, rent, or share any information with advertisers, third parties, or data brokers.
  • The only network request the Extension makes is the verification of the license key you enter (at activation and then at most once a day). Only the key is sent, to our payment provider Lemon Squeezy (api.lemonsqueezy.com), which tells the Extension whether the key is valid, expired or cancelled. No other server is contacted. If you don't use Pro, the Extension makes no network requests at all. As with any internet request, the receiving servers technically process standard connection data such as your IP address in order to respond.

💾 2. What the Extension Stores in Your Browser

Everything below stays in your browser's extension storage (chrome.storage) and never reaches our servers:

  • Preferences: speed, keyboard shortcuts, overlay settings, sites where shortcuts are disabled, speed rules.
  • Time-saved statistics: daily totals only (no list of videos or sites).
  • Video bookmarks: the timestamps and notes you save, keyed by the video's address.
  • License status: your license key and the result of its last verification.
  • Optional settings sync: if enabled (Options → Backup & sync), your preferences — not your license, statistics or bookmarks — are synced through your browser's own sync service (e.g. your Google, Microsoft or Mozilla account), under that provider's privacy policy.
  • Page access: the Extension interacts only with <video> and <audio> elements on the pages you visit, to change their speed locally. It does not read or transmit page content.

Uninstalling the Extension, or clearing its data, removes all of the above from your device.

📊 3. The Website: Analytics

To understand how many people visit this Website and which pages they read, the Website (not the Extension) uses Vercel Web Analytics, provided by Vercel Inc., which also hosts the Website.

  • It does not use cookies and does not store anything on your device.
  • It records page views with: the page visited, the referring website, country, browser, operating system and device type.
  • Visitors are not identified: page views are grouped by a hash of the request that is discarded every 24 hours, so you can't be followed across days or other websites. We don't use session recordings, heatmaps or advertising trackers.
  • We only see aggregated statistics. None of this data is connected to the Extension or to your license key.

See Vercel Web Analytics privacy and Vercel's privacy policy.

💳 4. Payments (Lemon Squeezy)

The 7-day free trial, the yearly Pro subscription (and the one-time licenses sold before it) are handled by our Merchant of Record, Lemon Squeezy, under its own privacy policy and terms. See also our Terms.

We never see, collect, or store credit card details, financial data, or billing addresses. Lemon Squeezy issues your license key and tells the Extension whether it is valid. As the seller, we can see in the Lemon Squeezy dashboard the order details it shares with us (such as your email address and subscription status), which we use only to provide the service and support.

⚖️ 5. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

The Extension does not send personal data to our servers, so there is no Extension data for us to view, export, modify, or delete: it all lives in your browser and you control it.

For any request about Website data (access, deletion, objection), contact us at the address below.
