Privacy Policy

Privacy Statement for the SpeedUP Video Pro browser extension and the website x2-speedup.vercel.app

Effective Date: October 2, 2026 | Last Updated: November 11, 2026

🔒 Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy covers two different things, which handle data differently:

  • The SpeedUP Video Pro browser extension ("the Extension") — works 100% locally in your browser and contains no analytics or tracking of any kind (Section 1).
  • This website, x2-speedup.vercel.app ("the Website") — uses third-party analytics to understand how visitors use the site (Section 3).

🚫 1. The Extension: Zero Tracking

The Extension does NOT collect, record, monitor, transmit, or sell personal data or browsing activity.

  • It does NOT include any analytics, telemetry, web beacons, or tracking scripts.
  • It does NOT send the websites you visit, video titles, or video URLs anywhere.
  • It does NOT sell, rent, or share any information with advertisers, third parties, or data brokers.
  • The only network request the Extension makes is the verification of the license key you enter (at activation and then at most once a day). Only the key is sent, to our license endpoint (x2-speedup.vercel.app) and to our payment provider Lemon Squeezy (api.lemonsqueezy.com). As with any internet request, the receiving servers technically process standard connection data such as your IP address in order to respond.

💾 2. What the Extension Stores in Your Browser

Everything below stays in your browser's extension storage (chrome.storage) and never reaches our servers:

  • Preferences: speed, keyboard shortcuts, overlay settings, sites where shortcuts are disabled, speed rules.
  • Time-saved statistics: daily totals only (no list of videos or sites).
  • Video bookmarks: the timestamps and notes you save, keyed by the video's address.
  • License status: your license key and the result of its last verification.
  • Optional settings sync: if enabled (Options → Backup & sync), your preferences — not your license, statistics or bookmarks — are synced through your browser's own sync service (e.g. your Google, Microsoft or Mozilla account), under that provider's privacy policy.
  • Page access: the Extension interacts only with <video> and <audio> elements on the pages you visit, to change their speed locally. It does not read or transmit page content.

Uninstalling the Extension, or clearing its data, removes all of the above from your device.

📊 3. The Website: Analytics

To understand how visitors use this Website and improve it, the Website (not the Extension) uses:

  • Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.) — pages visited, approximate location, device and browser type, how you arrived on the site.
  • Microsoft Clarity (Microsoft Corporation) — how visitors interact with the pages (clicks, scrolling, session replays and heatmaps).

These services may set cookies or similar technologies and process data under their own privacy policies: Google · Microsoft. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. None of this data is connected to the Extension or to your license key.

💳 4. Payments (Lemon Squeezy)

The free trial and license purchases are handled by our Merchant of Record, Lemon Squeezy, under its own privacy policy and terms.

We never see, collect, or store credit card details, financial data, or billing addresses. Lemon Squeezy issues your license key and tells us whether it is valid.

⚖️ 5. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

The Extension does not send personal data to our servers, so there is no Extension data for us to view, export, modify, or delete: it all lives in your browser and you control it.

For Website analytics data, and for any other request (access, deletion, objection), contact us at the address below; you can also exercise your rights directly with Google and Microsoft.

📝 6. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect product changes or legal requirements. Changes are posted on this page with an updated date.
