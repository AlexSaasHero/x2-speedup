How to Change Echo360 Playback Speed (Past 2x)

Echo360's player lets you change the playback speed from its own menu, up to 2x. To go faster, you need an extension that sets the video's speed directly and keeps it there, because Echo360's player tends to put its own speed back. Echo360's speed menu: 0.25x to 2x

According to Echo360's help center, the player plays video “at .25 (quarter) speed up to 2x speed, or anywhere in between, at .25 intervals”. You pick the value from the player's speed menu. Older versions of the player started at 0.5x, and your university's setup may look slightly different, but 2x is the top of the menu in all of them.

Why speed extensions often don't work on Echo360

Many students try a generic video speed extension and see Echo360 jump back to 1x after a few seconds, or as soon as they press Play. That's because Echo360's player keeps its own speed setting. In our testing, it writes that speed back to the video when you press Play, after a seek, and regularly during playback. An extension that sets the speed once loses it at the next write. Echo360's help center itself notes that the player “cannot support all 3rd party speed controller plugins”.

LectureSpeed is built for this. On Echo360, when the player tries to put its own speed back, LectureSpeed keeps yours, so the lecture doesn't drop to 1x after Play, a pause or a seek. It changes nothing on Echo360's side: it only controls the video in your browser.

How to change Echo360 playback speed past 2x (step by step)

  1. 1. Add LectureSpeed to Chrome

    It's free and needs no account. It also works in Edge. Pin it from the puzzle-piece menu so the popup is one click away.

  2. 2. Open your Echo360 lecture

    Start the recording in Echo360's player.

  3. 3. Press D to go faster, S to slow down

    Each press changes the speed by 0.1x, and R goes back to 1x. You can also open the popup and pick a speed.

  4. 4. Keep watching

    LectureSpeed keeps your speed through pause, play and seeks, so you set it once.

Echo360's speed button still says 1x?

That's normal. The speed button shows the value selected in Echo360's own menu, and LectureSpeed doesn't change that menu: it sets the speed of the video directly. The LectureSpeed badge, in the top-right corner of the video, and the popup show the real speed. If you choose a speed in Echo360's menu, LectureSpeed keeps yours; press R to go back to 1x and hand the speed back to Echo360's player.

What speed should you use?

In a UCLA study (Murphy et al., Applied Cognitive Psychology, 2022), comprehension dropped only slightly at 1.5x and 2x, and more at 2.5x. Start at 2x, add 0.1x with D while you can still follow, and slow down with S on the hard parts. There's more in our guide on the best speed to watch recorded lectures.
